About

I am a Senior Researcher at Ericsson Research in Stockholm, in the Sensing and Perception team of the IoT Technologies and Cyber-Physical Systems research area. I work on perception for mobile robots, with a focus on making it hold up in environments the models were never trained for.

I am currently working on Seamless indoor-outdoor mobile robot semantic perception and navigation with domain adaptation (SIMNAV), a Digital Futures Industrial Innovation Project between Ericsson Research and KTH Royal Institute of Technology, which I initiated and co-formulated in 2025.

I hold a PhD from Universidad Carlos III de Madrid (2021, cum laude) on scene understanding for indoor service robots, with research stays at the Institute of Robotics and Mechatronics of the German Aerospace Center (DLR), the Czech Institute of Informatics, Robotics and Cybernetics (CIIRC) at the Czech Technical University in Prague, and the Centre for Applied Autonomous Sensor Systems (AASS) at Örebro University. I also hold an MSc in Robotics and Automation from Universidad Carlos III de Madrid.

Research

  • Adaptive perception

    Domain adaptation and domain shift detection for open-vocabulary semantic segmentation, so that a robot's perception adapts as it moves between environments, and only when it needs to.

  • Perception on the robot

    Efficient inference and deployment of vision foundation models on embedded hardware, including offloading and resource-aware detection and tracking.

  • Semantic mapping and navigation

    Scene understanding, place recognition and localisation in changing indoor environments, the line of work from my PhD that the current research builds on.
